Chrome History Cleaner
Privacy Policy

Last updated November 12, 2026

Browsing history

The extension checks page addresses and titles against your rules. It uses visit times to scan your history. These checks run on your device. The extension does not transmit browsing history or use analytics. Chrome's own sync settings may affect where changes to your history are reflected.

Local storage

Your rules, schedule, and latest cleanup counts are stored in the extension's local storage. Chrome keeps preview URLs in session memory. A preview can authorize cleanup for up to ten minutes. Closing Chrome clears this session storage. Removing the extension clears its stored settings. Rules exported as a JSON file remain wherever you saved the file.

Support

Opening Support takes you to Google Forms. The extension does not fill in or send browsing history to the form. If you submit a message, Google Forms stores what you choose to include and the developer uses it to respond or investigate the issue. Please omit private URLs and other sensitive information. Include an email address only if you want a reply.

Ko-fi

Buy me a coffee opens Ko-fi. The extension does not send your rules or history to Ko-fi. Ko-fi handles any payment you choose to make.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

Chrome History Cleaner's use of information received from Chrome APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
